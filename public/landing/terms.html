Privacy Policy

01

What We Collect

We collect only what we need to operate Statsy:

  • Account data: your email address and password (hashed) when you sign up
  • Usage data: status pages, services, and incidents you create
  • Subscriber emails: emails collected on your public status page (these belong to you)
  • Billing data: handled entirely by Paddle — we never store your payment details
  • Log data: IP addresses, browser type, and pages visited for security and debugging
02

How We Use It

We use your data to:

  • Operate and improve the Statsy platform
  • Send transactional emails (account verification, incident notifications)
  • Process payments via Paddle

  • Detect and prevent abuse or fraud

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising.

03

Sharing Your Data

We share your data only with the third-party services required to run Statsy:
