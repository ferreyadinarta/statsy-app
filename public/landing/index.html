02 — Automated monitoring

Know before
your users do

Point Statsy at any URL. Every minute it checks: slow response, bad status code, or timeout. Status updates automatically and you get an email the moment something changes.

  • Checks every customizable interval on Pro (starting from 1 min), every 5 min on Free
  • Slow response (>2s) auto-marked as Degraded
  • 2 consecutive failures required before Outage, no false alarms
  • Email you and your subscribers the moment status changes
  • Custom check interval per service on Pro

How it works

Simple for you.
Clear for your users.

For you, the owner
1
Create your status page

Sign up, pick a name and a slug. Your page is live at yoursite.statsy.page in seconds.

2
Add your services

List the parts of your product: API, website, database, payments. Set each one to Operational, Degraded, or Outage.

3
Post incidents when things break

Write a quick update, change the status, and hit post. All subscribers get notified by email automatically.

4
Resolve and close

Once the incident is fixed, mark it resolved. Your subscribers get a final update and trust is restored.

For your users, the visitors
1
Visit your status page

They open your status link and instantly see which services are up, degraded, or down. No login needed.

2
Subscribe for updates

They enter their email once and subscribe. No account, no password. One click to stay informed.

3
Get notified by email

When you post an incident, they get an email immediately. No more angry tweets asking "is your site down?"

4
Know when it's resolved

They receive a resolution email when the incident is closed. They feel heard, informed, and trust you more.
